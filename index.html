About Xingsu

Wenzhou Xingsu Electronic Technology Co., Ltd.

Founded in 2015 in Pingyang, Wenzhou, Zhejiang, Xingsu integrates R&D, design, production and sales. The company operates an approximately 1,000 m² factory and provides OEM / ODM services for electronic and personal-care products.

The current product direction focuses on massage, scalp & hair care, beauty massage and related wellness devices for overseas brands, distributors and sourcing partners.
